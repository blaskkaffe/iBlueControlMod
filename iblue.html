Settings that only the iBlueControlMod firmware has:

  • Tap buttons: what a single, double and triple tap of the button do.
  • Profiles: four named copies of the whole BlueRetro configuration, to switch between with one click here or with a tap.

Disconnect all controllers from BlueRetro before connecting for configuration.

Tap buttons

Tap the button once, twice or three times (each tap within about half a second of the last). The single tap waits that half second to see if another tap follows.

Tap + hold (tap, then press again and keep holding) is not changeable: the LED sweeps through all colours, release when it shows the one you want and it is kept as the custom colour. The holds of 3, 6, 10 and 30 seconds are as in BlueRetro.

Profiles

A profile is a copy of the whole configuration: the mapping, output mode and accessories of every output, and the global settings. Save puts the configuration the adapter runs now into a slot; Load replaces the configuration with the profile and keeps it. Profiles survive a factory reset.
